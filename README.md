## Hi, I'm Konrad Fischer

I build things that live in the real world: hardware I wire and print myself, and software that people around me actually use.

### Projects

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**Court Vision**](https://github.com/Konradical1/court-vision) | Basketball shot tracker on a Raspberry Pi 5 with a Hailo AI accelerator, in a custom 3D-printed enclosure. Tracks the ball, calls makes and misses, and shows the session on your phone. |
| [**Jarvis Smart Mirror**](https://github.com/Konradical1/jarvis-smart-mirror) | Voice assistant built into a mirror in my room. Pi, Mac and phone apps sharing one assistant. Fourth version, after [V1](https://github.com/Konradical1/SmartMirror) and [V2](https://github.com/Konradical1/SmartMirrorV2). |
| [**SharedCut**](https://github.com/Konradical1/sharedcut-editor) | Collaborative video editor my film crew uses for real projects, plus an [MCP server](https://github.com/Konradical1/sharedcut-mcp) that lets AI agents edit timelines. |
| [**LifeSync**](https://github.com/Konradical1/lifesync) | iOS app for food logging and journaling with AI photo recognition. In public beta on TestFlight. |
| [**CAD Projects**](https://github.com/Konradical1/cad-projects) | Parametric 3D-printed parts generated in Python and sliced for my Bambu A1: camera organizer, sax wall mount, trophy, mirror mount. |

More about me and my films, photos and music: [konradfischer.com](https://konradfischer.com)

### How I work

I decide what to build, make the design calls, build the hardware, and test everything on real devices with real users. I use AI coding agents (Claude Code and OpenAI Codex) to write most of the code, and I'm open about it: you'll see their Co-Authored-By lines in the commit history, and the design specs they worked from are in the repos' `docs/` folders. The software projects also have a `HOW-IT-WORKS.md` explaining the main technical decisions.
